Fungi Tri

A 3-state take on Fungi. Each cell is empty, state 1 or state 2. The next state depends on the cell itself and on how many of its orthogonal and diagonal neighbors are in each state: 3 × 15 × 15 = 675 cases, so a rule is 675 trits written as 270 hex characters.

These rules were picked by a MoonBit search. It scored thousands of random and evolved rules for growth that keeps moving, without dying out, freezing, or boiling into noise. Every tile starts from the same seed: a disk of state 1 around a smaller disk of state 2, on a 96 × 96 grid that wraps at the edges.

Hover a tile to see its scores, and click it to open it full screen. Press Space to pause, R to restart, N to switch between the disk seed and a random seed, and Esc to return here. Add ?skip=300 to the address to jump ahead. This page needs WebGPU, which recent Chrome has.
